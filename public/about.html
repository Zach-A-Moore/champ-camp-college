About Champ Camp College

Socializing access: putting real operators and leaders in front of students, every week, for the price of a coffee.

Our mission

Most students never get to hear, first-hand, how a business is actually built and run. Champ Camp College brings business owners, executives and community leaders to students in a live weekly virtual talk, followed by open Q&A.

Talks are online, so anyone with a school email can join from anywhere. A single $5 registration covers every talk in the season. Once a year we meet in person at the Champ Camp community meetup.

What every talk covers

  • Strategies: how the speaker decides what to do and why.
  • Techniques: the specific methods, scripts and habits they use.
  • Execution: what doing the work actually looks like day to day.
  • Results: what worked, what didn't, and what they'd do differently.

Our founder

Najam Usmani moved to the United States at 19 and built a 25-year corporate and entrepreneurial career, hiring, training and mentoring more than 2,700 employees. He ran one of the top 50 franchises of the nation's second-largest tax preparation company for 14 years and is an active real estate developer in Charlotte, North Carolina.

A philanthropist and member of the Consular Corps College, he received the Mayoral International Community Award for his work with Champ Camp.
