Frequently asked questions

For students

How much does it cost?

$5 per student, once. That one registration covers every weekly talk this season. Payment is handled by Stripe, so we never see your card details.

Can I get a refund?

Cancelling stops talk emails but doesn't refund automatically. If you need a refund, reply to your confirmation email or contact us using the address in the footer.

Who can attend?

Talks are designed for college and graduate students, from any school and any major. Community members and professionals can join as guests.

How do I join a talk?

Register with your school email. We email the join link before each talk. You don't need to install anything ahead of time.

Do I need an account?

No. Registration only needs your name, email, major, year and the $5 payment. Logging in (with a code we email you) is optional and lets you see and manage your registrations in one place.

When are talks?

Weekly. Each talk's date and time is listed on the homepage, shown in your own time zone.

I didn't get my email.

Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure your school's email filter allows outside mail. You can also register again with the same address; we won't charge you twice, we'll just resend your confirmation.

How do I cancel or unsubscribe?

Use the cancel link in any confirmation email, or log in and go to My account.

Can I give feedback on a talk?

Yes. After each talk we share a short feedback form with attendees. It helps us pick future speakers.

For speakers and guests

What does speaking involve?

A 30 to 45 minute live, virtual talk on how you built or run something real, followed by student Q&A.

How are speakers chosen?

We review every application for fit with our student audience and email you either way. You can check your status in My account.
