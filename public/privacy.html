Privacy notice

Plain-language summary of what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Student registration: your name, email, school (optional), major, year in school, and whether your payment went through.
  • Payments: Stripe processes the $5 fee. We receive the amount and a payment reference, never your card number.
  • Speaker and guest applications: your name, email, and what you tell us about your talk.
  • Logging in: your email and when you logged in.
  • Security records: an activity log (for example, login attempts) with IP addresses, used to prevent abuse.

Why we use it

To send you confirmations and join links, to plan talks, to review applications, and to keep the site secure. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only Champ Camp College administrators. Stripe processes payments, our email provider delivers messages, and Cloudflare Turnstile runs the security check on our forms.

How long we keep it

Registrations and applications are kept for the current season and up to one year after, then deleted. Security logs are kept only as long as needed to protect the site.

Your choices

You can cancel registrations or withdraw an application any time from My account or the link in your email. To have your information deleted, contact us using the email address in the site footer.
